One form engine, three forms

The Level 1 wood stove checklist, a course signup and a concern report are the same schema-driven engine, not three hand-built screens. Add a form by writing a schema.

Signatures are drawn on the glass. The checklist ends in a PDF the technician sends from the device. Find searches an invented directory of 55 people across every provincial and territorial capital — real designations, real geography, invented names, companies and contact details. The app records what the inspector enters and never rates compliance itself.

All three frames run the same build. Each is its own session, so what you type in one stays in that one. Tap around.
